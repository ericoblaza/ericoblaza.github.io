I am Erico Blaza, a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology (BSIT) student at the University of San Jose-Recoletos in Cebu, Philippines. I am an aspiring full-stack web developer focused on building useful, well-structured websites and web applications.

I enjoy learning by shipping small projects — from clean landing pages to PHP apps that work with a MySQL database. I am still early in my career, and I treat that as a strength: I ask questions, practice often, and care about writing code I can explain.

Right now I am deepening my skills in frontend fundamentals and PHP backends, and I am open to internships, junior roles, and collaborative projects where I can contribute and keep learning.
